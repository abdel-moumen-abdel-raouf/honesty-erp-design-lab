## Adopted characteristics

- Horizontal underline and pills variants.
- Content-sized and equal/fill distribution.
- Vertical tabs with logical start/end placement.
- Text, icon-and-text, and accessible icon-only headers.
- Bounded panel transitions: `none | fade | fade-up | fade-down | fade-start | fade-end`.
- Clear active state and disabled state.

Tablist/tab/tabpanel semantics, Home/End, orientation-correct arrows, logical RTL horizontal reversal, rich keyed panels, Component Tokens, and reduced-motion resolution remain Honesty ERP-owned. Product Owner runtime/visual approval remains pending.
